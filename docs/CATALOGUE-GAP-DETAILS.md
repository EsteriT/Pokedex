- 21,256 supplied card entries; 1,590 lack an image URL in the source snapshot.

- All 348 image-gap cards in those four groups therefore have potential metadata counterparts. The remaining 1,242 belong to other groups. Four other groups received a strict TCGCSV check; no provider-wide absence claim is established.
