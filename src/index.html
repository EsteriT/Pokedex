A few things to know.

Quick answers before you start.

Is this a digital or physical binder?

A digital binder. Arrange cards online and track the cards you own.

Can I keep my collection private?

Your saved binders stay in this browser. Account saves and private sharing come later.

Can I print my designs?

Not yet. Image exports, PDF and print layouts are planned.
